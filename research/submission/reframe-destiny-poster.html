Introduction

Young people absorb gender roles from fate-talk: the same chart praises men as “ambitious” and women as “ke-fu” or “too strong.” We need reframing, not only prediction.

RQ: How does an AI-assisted site affect ability to identify and reframe gendered divination bias?

Literature

  • Gender as performative script (Butler, 1990)
  • Discourse & power (Foucault, 1972)
  • AI oracles as authority (Bender et al., 2021; Cotter, 2022)

Method

Within-subjects pre/post · N = 19 paired (34 sessions, 23 journeys) · 5 interviews

Sites: pages.dev/game + vercel.app/game

6 steps: choose chart → birth data (local) → traditional read → bias scanner → Court of Destiny → 3-column compare

Limitations

Implications
