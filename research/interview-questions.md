# Semi-Structured Interview Protocol · Reframe Destiny

**Who:** 5 participants drawn from post-survey completers (paired sample)  
**When:** After they finish pre → journey → post (same week)  
**Format:** 10–15 min · WeChat voice or text · voluntary · anonymous in paper  
**RQ alignment:** identify + reframe gendered bias in BaZi/astrology narratives

---

## Five core questions (English · for paper Methods)

Ask in order; follow up with “Can you give an example from your session?”

### Q1 · Baseline identification (→ *bias_awareness*, *spot_stereotypes*)

**Before** you used Reframe Destiny, when you saw gendered words in BaZi or astrology—such as *ke-fu*, “late marriage,” “too strong,” or virtuous-wife tropes—did you read them as normal fate-talk or as something biased? **After** the traditional reading and bias scanner, did anything feel different?

**中文大意：** 用网站之前，看到「克夫」「晚婚」「太强势」等说法，你觉得正常还是偏见？读完传统解读、用完偏见扫描器之后，有没有不一样？

**Why it matters:** Separates pre-existing critique from session-induced noticing; directly tests whether the tool changes *identification*.

---

### Q2 · Which step taught “seeing” (→ journey design / IV)

Which step most helped you **notice** gender bias—the traditional reading, the bias tags/scanner, the Court of Destiny, or the side-by-side reframe? **Why that step?**

**中文大意：** 哪一步最帮你「看见」偏见——传统解读、偏见标签/扫描器、命运法庭，还是三栏对照？为什么？

**Why it matters:** Tells you which part of the 6-step loop actually drives awareness (design evidence for Discussion).

---

### Q3 · Reframing vs. replacing authority (→ *reframe_confidence*, AI disclosure)

You saw traditional, modern, and AI-reframed versions. Which version helped you **question** the original reading—not just swap one expert for another? When you wrote your one line in the Court of Destiny, did that feel like **your** rewrite or mostly agreeing with the AI?

**中文大意：** 传统/现代/AI 三版里，哪一版最帮你「质疑」原文而不是换一个新权威？命运法庭那句是你自己的改写，还是更像同意 AI？

**Why it matters:** Core to RQ (“reframe”) and to the agency/AI-authority tension in your data.

---

### Q4 · Gender lens (→ *gender_lens*)

If the **same chart** were explained to someone of a **different gender**, do you think the traditional wording would change? Did the site make you ask that question for the first time, or had you already wondered?

**中文大意：** 同一张盘换个性别，传统说法会变吗？是网站让你第一次想到这个问题，还是你以前就想过？

**Why it matters:** Directly maps to the gender_lens Likert item and Butler/Foucault “who is the addressee?” argument.

---

### Q5 · Overall impact + redesign (→ RQ + limitations)

In **one sentence**: did this ~15-minute journey change how well you can **spot** or **rewrite** gendered fate-talk? If you could **keep one step and remove one step** to help other young people talk back, which would you choose?

**中文大意：** 用一句话说：这 15 分钟有没有改变你识别或改写性别化命运话术的能力？只能留一步、删一步，你会留什么、删什么？

**Why it matters:** Closes the loop on the full RQ; yields quotable lines for Discussion and future design.

---

## Optional probes (if they give short answers)

- “Can you point to a **specific phrase** in your reading that felt gendered?”
- “Did the AI reframe feel like **fortune-telling** or **teaching**?”
- “Would you use this site again—or share it—and why or why not?”
